# Loom script (under 60 seconds)

**0–5s.** "Brands come to Collabstr with an idea but no brief. This turns four choices into a usable one."

**5–20s.** Type "Glow Lab". Click TikTok, Awareness, Playful. Add "new vitamin C serum for sensitive skin". Click **Generate brief**. While the skeleton shows: "One call to Claude with a strict JSON schema, checked again on the server."

**20–40s.** Scroll the result: summary, objective, deliverables, do's/don'ts, content angles with hooks, creator criteria. Click into a hook and change a word.

**40–55s.** Click **Copy brief** and paste into a notes app. "The one interaction I chose is edit plus copy. The brief's next stop is a campaign form or a creator DM, so copying the edited text is the shortest path, and it's my success metric: briefs copied divided by briefs generated."

**55–60s.** "Next step: pre-fill Collabstr's campaign form directly."
